# Recruitment Bot

## What it is
A private C# bot sample from 2022 for cutting recruiter screening work. The solution is BotSamplesDev.sln with a DialogBotDev project, built on the Bot Framework dialog model.

## What it was for
- Candidate intake and routing through dialogs.
- FAQ and knowledge-base answers for common screening questions.
- A place to try admin-style handoff before a full ATS.

## Stack
C#, Bot Framework, dialog-based conversation flow.

## Where it led
The same idea later became JobFusion and the Job Farming Command Center: search, tailor, track, and keep a human in the loop before anything is sent.
